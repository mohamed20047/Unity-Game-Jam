## Long description
The flashy bits for your RPG, roguelike or action game: arrows and spells that fly in 8 directions, hits, explosions, sword slashes, healing, shields, teleports and status effects. Frames are 16x16 (the large explosion and the level-up burst are 32x32), with a 1px dark outline, hard pixels (alpha only fully on or off) and the same 32-colour palette as our [16x16 Characters & Monsters](https://larchrow.itch.io/larchrow-16x16-characters-monsters), [RPG Item Icons](https://larchrow.itch.io/larchrow-rpg-item-icons) and tilesets, so it all looks like one game.

### What you get
- **Projectiles (7):** arrow, fireball, ice shard and magic bolt in **8 directions**; spinning rock, pulsing energy orb, spinning throwing knife
- **Impacts (8):** hit spark, fire burst, ice shatter, magic burst, lightning strike and explosions in **small, medium and large (32x32)**
- **Melee (2):** sword slash in 4 directions, claw swipe
- **Magic (5):** heal sparkle, magic aura, shield bubble, teleport out/in, level-up burst (32x32)
- **Status loops (4):** poison, burn, freeze, stun
- **Ambient (5):** smoke puff, dust step, water splash, pickup sparkle, coin glint
- **63 animations, 270 frames** in total
- PNG strip per animation (no Aseprite needed), one sheet per effect and combined sheets, each at 1x and 3x
- `animation_index.csv` / `.json`: frame size, frames, suggested fps, loop flag and pixel position of every frame
- **Godot 4:** one ready SpriteFrames `.tres` per effect (fps and loop already set) and a demo scene where a fireball flies and explodes (tested with Godot 4.3)
- Animated GIF preview of every effect, palette as .png, .gpl and .hex

### Made with code, no AI
Every effect is drawn by our own Python code: pixel templates, flame and ring rules, radial heat bands for the explosions and soft cloud shading for smoke and dust. Then every frame was looked at and fixed by eye. The characters and maps in the screenshots are from our other packs and are not part of this download.

### Checked automatically (qa-report.json in the download)
- Frame sizes as documented, 3x sheets are exact nearest-neighbour copies
- Only the 32 palette colours; no semi-transparent pixels
- Every frame has a closed 1px outer outline, is not empty and does not touch the frame edge; no animation is static
- Frame counts consistent; the 8 directions are exact rotations, slash left mirrors right
- Strips, sheets, index and Godot files match; all SpriteFrames and the demo scene load in Godot 4.3
